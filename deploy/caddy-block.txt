Add this site block to the Caddyfile ConfigMap in homelab/infra/k8s/caddy.yaml
(alongside the other app blocks), then redeploy Caddy. WebSockets work with no
extra directives — Caddy's reverse_proxy forwards Upgrade/Connection headers.

werewolf.example.com {
    reverse_proxy werewolf.homelab.svc.cluster.local:3000
}
